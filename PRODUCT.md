# ALFA GLASS A.E.

register: brand

## Product purpose
Corporate and catalogue website for ALFA GLASS A.E., an importer and wholesaler of flat glass (float, decorative, mirrors, energy, reflective, coated, acoustic, safety, fire-resistant), plastic sheets (acrylic, polycarbonate, PET-G, PVC, Bond panels) and related products (glass testing devices, glass-door hardware, machine consumables and tools). Founded 1999, based in Aspropyrgos (Θέση Κύριλλος, exit 4 of Attiki Odos) in an owned 13.000 m² warehouse and office building.

## Users
- Professionals of the glass trade: glaziers, glass processors, aluminium and frame fabricators, sign makers (plastics). They need to see the range, read technical notes, and call or email sales fast.
- Architects, engineers and contractors who specify glass and want credible technical content (standards such as EN 356, EN 1063, EN 12543).
- Prospective partners and suppliers judging the company's scale and seriousness.

## Brand and tone
Heavy, exact, transparent. A serious B2B stockist with 25+ years, big inventory and real infrastructure, presented with the confidence of a premium manufacturer. Greek first; English later.
Tagline from the legacy site: «Τα πάντα για το γυαλί!» Promise: «Είμαστε δίπλα στον επαγγελματία για να καλύψουμε κάθε του ανάγκη.»

Identity to preserve: logo mark (cyan-to-blue "a" in a ring) and wordmark in deep indigo #281a6a; blue building facade.

## References
- muskita.com.cy (cinematic dark, full-bleed imagery, split image/text sections) as the mood reference the client gave.

## Anti-references
- Generic Bootstrap corporate template (the legacy site): grey boxes, "Περισσότερα" buttons everywhere, carousel sliders, tiny type.
- SaaS landing clichés: hero metrics, gradient text, identical icon cards.

## Content constraints
Keep the legacy content (all categories, 75 products, company pages, history, financial statements PDFs, legal pages, ESPA banner). Copy can be tightened but not invented.
